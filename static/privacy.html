Privacy

Your words are yours.

Litteram is built like a tool, not a platform. No account, no cloud, no tracking - just Markdown files on your Mac.

▣

Everything stays on your Mac

Your documents live exactly where you put them. No uploads, no cloud, no remote servers.

◌

No tracking or analytics

We don't collect usage data, crash reports, or telemetry. What you write stays private.

◈

No account required

Download, unzip, and write. There's no login, subscription, or gatekeeping.

⌑

Open source by design

Released under GPLv3, the full source is available to inspect, audit, and improve.

▤

Plain Markdown forever

Your work is stored in standard .md files you can open with any text editor.

◇

You control the sync

Use iCloud, Dropbox, or git whenever you want. Litteram never touches the network.
